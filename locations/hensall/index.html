FAQs

Hensall Duct Cleaning Questions

Could ducts contribute to recurring household dust?

They may be one possible source. Note which rooms and registers are affected, check accessible return ducts for visible gaps, and photograph what you find. An assessment can help distinguish duct buildup or leakage from dust entering by other routes.

What should I ask about dryer-vent cleaning?

Confirm whether the scope follows the whole vent from the dryer to its exterior hood, including bends and longer runs. Ask whether the hood will be checked and whether airflow will be measured at the termination, with readings recorded for comparison.

Can an HRV be included in a project assessment?

If the property has an HRV, provide its make and model and ask whether the scope includes the core, screens, and condensate path. You can also request filter sizes, locations, and maintenance guidance that reflects the equipment instructions.

What information helps when contacting someone from a farm property?

Share the location, preferred contact method and times, equipment involved, and any access constraints. Photos of labels, the dryer outlet, or accessible ductwork can help clarify the project. Scheduling and availability should be confirmed directly rather than assumed.
