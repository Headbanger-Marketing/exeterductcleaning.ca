FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

How often do Huron County homes need duct cleaning?

Around here, every two years is the honest baseline, and farm properties on gravel roads often benefit from a shorter cycle. Harvest dust, renovation debris, and pets all pull the schedule toward sooner rather than later.

Are your people certified and insured?

Both, on every job, no exceptions. Duct cleaning has no government certification scheme in Ontario, so the things worth checking are technician training, certification, and insurance, and we put all three in writing.

What proof do I get that the work was done?

Camera photos of your actual duct interiors, taken before the brushes touch anything and again after, reviewed with you on site. Around Exeter the befores are usually convincing and the afters are usually a relief.

Our dryer heats but nothing dries. What is that?

Classic lint-blocked vent, especially with farm laundry volumes. We brush the full pipe to the exterior hood and measure airflow at the end. If the numbers say the machine itself is tired, we say so instead of selling you a second cleaning.

What happens during a whole-home cleaning?

Negative-pressure vacuum on the trunk lines, registers off and washed, every supply and return branch brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, photos at start and finish. Figure three to four hours for a typical Exeter house.

The vents smell when the furnace starts in fall. Can you fix that?

Yes. The odour is residue, must from a damp spring, pest traffic, or cooking decades. Cleaning removes most of it and a sanitizer treatment on the clean ducts handles the remainder. Treating before cleaning accomplishes nothing, so we never do it.

Why does our house go dusty a day after cleaning it?

Leaking return ducts in the basement or crawlspace, most often. They feed dusty air straight to the blower and out every register. We can test for leaks and seal them, which is usually the end of the re-dusting cycle.

Do you serve the small communities around Exeter?

We do, all of South Huron and beyond, including Grand Bend, Bayfield, Seaforth, Clinton, Zurich, and Hensall, plus rural properties in between.
